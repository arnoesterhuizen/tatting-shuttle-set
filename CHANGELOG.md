# Changelog

All notable changes to the Tatting Shuttle Set. Dates in ISO format (YYYY-MM-DD).

## [v16] - 2026-11-13

First public release.

- Parametric shuttle (length and width), fixed standard bobbin (22 mm flange, 6.1 mm bore, ~11.2 mm height), and five picot gauges (2/4/6/8/10 mm).
- Geometric bobbin press-fit, layer-snapped vertical dimensions, CA-glued blades.
- Includes Fusion `.f3d` and STEP source, a print-ready `.3mf` (0.4 mm nozzle / 0.12 mm layer height), assembly instructions (PDF + SVG), and a full parameter reference.
